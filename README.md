# IADAI-203_2505204-Phreya_Shethwala
Digital GreenWall is an interactive prototype that helps students connect with nature through daily eco-challenges, virtual gardening, and team missions. Students earn EcoPoints, grow a plant avatar, and unlock rewards as they log their activities.

# Project overview and purpose
Digital GreenWall encourages students to notice nature in their daily routines. A student can care for a plant, spot a bird or sketch a leaf, then log what they did. Each recorded action adds to their progress and helps grow a virtual plant. The aim is to make small outdoor activities easy to start and give students a reason to return.

# Key features
- Daily eco-challenges — Filter by Easy, Medium or Hard. Record a short description and optionally add a photo. Each challenge awards points once per day.

- AR Green Viewer — Place a seed pot on a virtual desk, water it and add sunlight to see it grow. Restart to try again.

- Progress dashboard — My GreenWall shows EcoPoints, badges and activity history. The avatar starts as a Seedling, becomes a Sprout at three actions and a Sapling at five.

- Team missions and leaderboard — Join Moss Makers and record a plant, bird or insect you observed in Campus Nature Watch. The observation updates the team goal and simulated leaderboard.

- Rewards and recognition — Earn the First Leaf badge after one action and the Sapling badge after five. Unlock Golden Pot at 40 points, Sage Ribbon at 75 and Garden Star at 120. Equipping an accessory preserves the points balance.

# App flow and screen walkthrough
- Welcome — Enter a first name, set a gender preference, enter an age, and select Continue to GreenWall.

- Home — Choose a plant avatar and interests, check your balance, or open a suggested challenge.

- Challenges — Pick a difficulty and select Start mission on a task.

- Mission form — Describe the action, optionally attach a photo, and select Record mission. The app opens My GreenWall with a confirmation.

- My GreenWall — Check the updated balance, plant growth, badges and activity history.

- AR Viewer — Follow the three actions to grow a virtual plant.

- Teams — Join Moss Makers and record a nature observation. Check the updated team contribution.

- Rewards — Choose an unlocked accessory and see it on the plant on Home and My GreenWall.

- Profile — Edit personal details, avatar and interests while keeping existing progress.

Example walkthrough: Complete an Easy challenge for 20 EcoPoints. Join Moss Makers and complete Campus Nature Watch for 40 more. Check that My GreenWall shows 60 EcoPoints and that Moss Makers progresses from 6/10 to 7/10. Equip Golden Pot and return to Home to see it on your plant.

# Design and prototype behaviour
The design uses ivory backgrounds, forest-green buttons, sage highlights and muted gold rewards. Rounded cards group related information, while labels such as Completed, Locked and Equipped show the status of each action.

Home recommends an unfinished challenge that matches a selected interest when one is available. The AR scene and team leaderboard use simulated data. Mission records are self-reported, and EcoPoints track participation. The web demo stores profile details and progress in the browser.
